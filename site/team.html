Meet the Team

Alpine Robotics is a student-run FRC team supported by volunteer mentors. Every season, students take the lead on designing, building, wiring, programming, and driving a competition robot — while also running our outreach, fundraising, and media efforts.

Our team is organized into four sub-teams: Mechanical, Electrical, Programming, and Business/Outreach. New members are welcome on any sub-team, no prior experience required — mentors and returning students run workshops every preseason to get everyone up to speed.
