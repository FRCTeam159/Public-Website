Outreach

Part of our mission is bringing STEM and FIRST Robotics to our wider community. Below are some of our recent events — add a new post for each new event, right at the top of the list.

Recent Community Involvement

Elementary School STEM Night

Students brought last year's competition robot to our local elementary school's STEM night, running driving demos and letting kids try the controls under supervision. We talked with over 100 students and families about FIRST Robotics and how to get involved.

Community Robotics Workshop

We hosted a free introductory workshop covering basic mechanical assembly and block-based programming for middle schoolers interested in future FIRST programs (FLL and FTC).

Local Food Bank Volunteering

Beyond STEM outreach, the team spent a Saturday sorting and packing donations at our local food bank as part of our broader community service commitment.
